Reproduce with `python run_experiments.py --trials 5 --packets 100 --seed 17 --nodes 6`; new CSV outputs are ignored by default. Snapshot may not match current source/dependency versions; inspect trial-level data and regenerate before citing.

No INET `.sca`, `.vec`, `.rt` outputs were present in source audit. INET results, custom-forwarding diagnostics, and hardware measurements are not verified/present. Do not treat the unbuilt INET configs as experiment outcomes.
